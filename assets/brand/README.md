# Brand assets

Optional files the pipeline picks up automatically:

- `music.mp3` — background bed, mixed in at `style.music_volume` and faded out
  at the end. Omit it and videos ship with voice only.
- `logo.png` — reserved for a future end-card variant.

Gitignored. Use audio you have the right to use; music is the most common way
a Short gets a copyright claim.
